  • Tickets: keep flight or train details with the trip so they are ready offline.

Common questions

Is TravelReadyGo free?

You can create one trip for free. Yearly and Lifetime unlocks remove the trip limit via Apple In-App Purchase.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. See the Privacy Policy.

Camera or photo access was denied

Open iOS Settings → TravelReadyGo and allow Camera / Photos if you want to attach images. The app works without them.

OCR suggestions look wrong

Suggestions are optional. Edit fields manually, or retake a clearer photo. Nothing is uploaded for recognition.

Restore purchases

Use Restore on the paywall or in Settings after signing in with the same Apple ID used to buy.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

常见问题

TravelReadyGo 免费吗？

可以免费创建 1 个行程。年订阅或终身买断可解除次数限制，通过 Apple 应用内购买完成。

我的数据存在哪？

在本机设备上，详见隐私政策。

相机或相册权限被拒绝

到 iOS「设置 → TravelReadyGo」开启相机 / 照片即可。不授权也能正常使用其余功能。

OCR 识别不准

识别结果可忽略，也可手动改。换一张更清晰的照片重试。识别在本机完成，不会上传。

恢复购买

使用购买时的同一 Apple ID，在付费墙或设置里点「恢复购买」。

退款

购买由 Apple 处理，请到 reportaproblem.apple.com 申请。
