Privacy Policy

TravelReadyGo · Last updated: 1 November 2026
Short version: TravelReadyGo keeps your trips, checklists, tickets, and photos on your device. We do not run an account system and do not upload your personal trip data to our servers.

1. What TravelReadyGo is

TravelReadyGo is an iOS travel preparation app. You create trips, manage packing and entry checklists, keep ticket notes, and optionally attach photos (for example boarding passes). On-device OCR can suggest text from photos you choose; you decide whether to apply those suggestions.

2. Data we do not collect

  • No account, no sign-up, no email required to use the app
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No sale of personal data

3. Data stored on your device

Trip details, checklist progress, ticket notes, passport nationality preference, language preference, and photos you attach are stored locally in the app sandbox (including Application Support). Deleting the app removes this data. We do not operate a TravelReadyGo cloud backup of your content.

4. Camera and photo library

If you choose to take a photo or pick one from your library, the image stays on your device for that checklist or ticket item. On-device Vision OCR may read text from the image to suggest fields. Photos are not uploaded to us.

5. Purchases

Optional Yearly and Lifetime unlocks are sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes payment. We receive only aggregate sales and subscription reports from Apple and never see your payment card details.

6. Diagnostics

If you enable crash reporting on your device, Apple may share anonymized crash diagnostics with the developer. These reports are used only to fix defects. You can control this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

7. Children

TravelReadyGo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Your rights

Because trip content is stored on your device, uninstalling TravelReadyGo deletes the app’s local data. Purchase history and refunds are managed by Apple.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

10. Contact

隐私政策

TravelReadyGo · 最后更新：2026 年 11 月 1 日
一句话版本：行程、清单、票务和照片都保存在你的设备上。我们没有账号体系，也不会把你的行程内容上传到我们的服务器。

1. 产品性质

TravelReadyGo 是 iOS 出行准备应用。你可以创建行程、管理行李与入境清单、记录票务，并可选地为清单或票务附加照片。 设备端 OCR 可从你选择的照片中识别文字并给出建议，是否采用由你决定。

2. 我们不收集的数据

  • 没有账号，使用 App 无需注册或邮箱
  • 没有统计或广告 SDK
  • 不做跨 App / 网站追踪
  • 不出售个人数据

3. 保存在你设备上的数据

行程信息、清单进度、票务备注、护照国籍偏好、语言偏好，以及你附加的照片，都保存在 App 沙盒（含 Application Support）内。 删除 App 即清除。我们不提供 TravelReadyGo 云端内容备份。

4. 相机与相册

当你主动拍照或从相册选择图片时，照片仅用于对应清单或票务条目，并留在本机。设备端 Vision OCR 可能读取图片文字以提供建议。 照片不会上传给我们。

5. 购买

可选的年订阅与终身买断通过 Apple 应用内购买完成，支付由 Apple 处理。我们只从 Apple 获得汇总的销售 / 订阅报表，看不到你的支付卡信息。

6. 诊断数据

若你在设备上开启了崩溃共享，Apple 可能向开发者提供匿名崩溃诊断，仅用于修复问题。可在 iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 分析与改进」中管理。

7. 儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不会有意收集儿童的个人数据。

8. 你的权利

行程等内容保存在本机，卸载 TravelReadyGo 即可删除 App 本地数据。购买记录与退款由 Apple 处理。

9. 政策更新

如政策有变化，更新版本会发布在本页并标注新日期。
