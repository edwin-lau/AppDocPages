Privacy Policy

TaxScanBag · Last updated: 7 November 2026
Short version: TaxScanBag scans and organizes PDFs on your device. We do not run an account system and do not upload your documents to our servers.

1. What TaxScanBag is

TaxScanBag is an iOS document scanner and PDF toolkit. You can scan paper with the camera, import files, organize scene bags (for example tax, visa/lease, and privacy workflows), merge, split, extract, compress, sign, or password-protect PDFs, and keep a local library and drafts — all without sending documents to a developer backend.

2. Data we do not collect

  • No account, no sign-up, no email required to use the app
  • No analytics or advertising SDKs
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No sale of personal data
  • No upload of your scans or PDFs to our servers

3. Data stored on your device

Scanned pages, imported PDFs, scene bags, drafts, library items, export preferences, language preference, and related working files are stored locally in the app sandbox. Deleting the app removes this data. We do not operate a TaxScanBag cloud backup of your content.

4. Camera, photos, and files

If you choose to scan with the camera, pick photos, or import documents from Files, those files stay on your device for processing inside TaxScanBag. On-device OCR may be used to make searchable PDFs. Content is not uploaded to us. You may share finished PDFs yourself through the system share sheet; destinations you pick (Mail, Files, other apps) are controlled by you and Apple, not by a TaxScanBag server.

5. Purchases

Optional Lifetime unlock is sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes payment. We receive only aggregate sales reports from Apple and never see your payment card details.

6. Diagnostics

If you enable crash reporting on your device, Apple may share anonymized crash diagnostics with the developer. These reports are used only to fix defects. You can control this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

7. Children

TaxScanBag is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Your rights

Because documents are stored on your device, uninstalling TaxScanBag deletes the app’s local data. Purchase history and refunds are managed by Apple.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

10. Contact

隐私政策

TaxScanBag · 最后更新：2026 年 11 月 7 日
一句话版本：扫描与 PDF 都保存在你的设备上。我们没有账号体系，也不会把你的文档上传到我们的服务器。

1. 产品性质

TaxScanBag 是 iOS 文档扫描与 PDF 工具箱。你可以用相机扫描、导入文件、整理场景袋（如税务、签证/租房、隐私流程）， 合并、拆分、抽取、压缩、签名或为 PDF 设置打开密码，并在本机保存资料库与草稿。全程不向开发者后端上传文档。

2. 我们不收集的数据

  • 没有账号，使用 App 无需注册或邮箱
  • 没有统计或广告 SDK
  • 不做跨 App / 网站追踪
  • 不出售个人数据
  • 不会把扫描件或 PDF 上传到我们的服务器

3. 保存在你设备上的数据

扫描页、导入的 PDF、场景袋、草稿、资料库、导出偏好、语言偏好及相关工作文件，都保存在 App 沙盒内。 删除 App 即清除。我们不提供 TaxScanBag 云端内容备份。

4. 相机、相册与文件

当你主动扫描、选图或从「文件」导入时，内容仅在本机由 TaxScanBag 处理。设备端 OCR 可用于生成可搜索 PDF。 内容不会上传给我们。你可通过系统分享面板自行发送成品 PDF；目标 App（邮件、文件等）由你和 Apple 控制， 不是 TaxScanBag 服务器。

5. 购买

可选的终身买断通过 Apple 应用内购买完成，支付由 Apple 处理。我们只从 Apple 获得汇总销售报表，看不到你的支付卡信息。

6. 诊断数据

若你在设备上开启了崩溃共享，Apple 可能向开发者提供匿名崩溃诊断，仅用于修复问题。可在 iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 分析与改进」中管理。

7. 儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不会有意收集儿童的个人数据。

8. 你的权利

文档保存在本机，卸载 TaxScanBag 即可删除 App 本地数据。购买记录与退款由 Apple 处理。

9. 政策更新

如政策有变化，更新版本会发布在本页并标注新日期。
