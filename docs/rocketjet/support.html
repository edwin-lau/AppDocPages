Getting started

  • Add a subscription: tap “Scan QR” or “Paste Link” at the bottom of the Home screen. You can also type a URL manually.
  • Choose a node: open the Subscriptions tab, expand the subscription in use, and tap a node.
  • Connect: return to Home and tap “Connect”. The ring shows the live status and traffic.

Common questions

RocketJet doesn’t include servers?

Correct. RocketJet is a client only. You need your own subscription link or node from a provider you trust.

Nothing happens when I tap Connect

Make sure you have at least one subscription added and a node selected, and allow the VPN configuration prompt the first time. If it still fails, check that your subscription is still valid and refresh it.

A subscription failed to import

Check that the link is reachable in Safari on the same device. Supported formats: Shadowsocks, VMess, VLESS and Trojan, as plain URIs, base64 subscription lists, or sing-box JSON.

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device. We run no servers and collect no data. See the Privacy Policy.

Does the Control Center button work without opening the app?

Yes on iOS 18 and later. If iOS blocks the extension from starting the tunnel, tapping the control opens the app and connects automatically.

常见问题

RocketJet 自带服务器吗？

不带。它是纯客户端，需要你自己准备订阅链接或节点。

点「开始加速」没反应

确认已经添加订阅并选中节点；首次连接要允许系统添加 VPN 配置。如果仍失败，刷新一下订阅，确认它没有过期。

订阅导入失败

先用 Safari 在这台设备上打开该链接，确认能访问。支持 Shadowsocks、VMess、VLESS、Trojan 的 URI 列表、base64 订阅，以及 sing-box JSON。

我的数据存在哪？

全部在本地设备上。我们没有服务器，也不收集数据，详见隐私政策。

控制中心按钮不用打开 App 也能用吗？

iOS 18 及以上可以。如果系统不允许扩展直接启动隧道，点控件会打开 App 并自动连接。
