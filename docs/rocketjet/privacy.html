Privacy Policy

RocketJet · Last updated: 22 October 2026
Short version: RocketJet is a client app. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data. Your subscriptions and settings stay on your device.

1. What RocketJet is

RocketJet is a proxy/VPN client for iOS. It does not provide servers, nodes, or subscription content. You import your own subscriptions or nodes. All proxied traffic goes directly between your device and the servers you configure — it never passes through us, because we operate no servers.

2. Data we do not collect

  • No account, no sign-up, no email required
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No advertising SDKs
  • No browsing history, traffic logs, or connection logs
  • No device identifiers or location data

3. Data stored on your device

Your subscriptions, node lists, selected node, and preferences (language, log level, proxy mode, DNS) are stored locally in the app's sandbox. Deleting the app removes them. They are not backed up to us.

4. Clipboard and camera

If you choose “Paste Link”, the app reads the clipboard once, on your explicit action, to import a subscription or node link. If you choose “Scan QR”, the app uses the camera (or a photo you pick) to recognize a QR code. Neither is used for anything else, and nothing is uploaded.

5. Diagnostics

If crash diagnostics are enabled on your device, Apple may share anonymized crash reports with the developer through Apple's MetricKit. These reports contain no personal data and are used only to fix defects. You can disable sharing in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

6. Purchases

RocketJet is a paid app. Payment is processed entirely by Apple. We receive only aggregate sales reports from Apple; we never see your payment details.

7. Children

RocketJet is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to export, correct, or delete on our side. Uninstalling the app removes all data the app stored locally.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

10. Contact

Questions: Support page · GitHub Issues

隐私政策

RocketJet · 最后更新：2026 年 10 月 22 日
一句话版本：RocketJet 是纯客户端应用，我们不收集、不存储、不传输你的任何个人数据。 订阅和设置只保存在你自己的设备上。

1. 产品性质

RocketJet 是 iOS 上的代理 / VPN 客户端，本身不提供服务器、节点或订阅内容。你需要自行导入订阅或节点。 所有代理流量都在你的设备与你配置的服务器之间直接传输，不经过我们——因为我们没有运营任何服务器。

2. 我们不收集的数据

  • 没有账号体系，不需要注册或邮箱
  • 没有统计或追踪 SDK
  • 没有广告 SDK
  • 不记录浏览历史、流量日志、连接日志
  • 不收集设备标识或位置信息

3. 保存在你设备上的数据

订阅、节点列表、当前选中节点，以及设置项（语言、日志级别、代理模式、DNS）都保存在 App 沙盒内。 删除 App 即全部清除，不会回传给我们。

4. 剪贴板与相机

当你主动点击「粘贴链接」时，App 会读取一次剪贴板，用于导入订阅或节点链接；点击「扫描二维码」时， App 会使用相机（或你选择的相册图片）识别二维码。两者都只用于该目的，不会上传任何内容。

5. 诊断数据

如果你的设备开启了崩溃共享，Apple 可能通过 MetricKit 向开发者提供匿名崩溃报告。这类报告不含个人信息， 仅用于修复缺陷。你可以在 iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 分析与改进」中关闭。

6. 购买

RocketJet 是付费应用，支付完全由 Apple 处理。我们只从 Apple 获得汇总的销售报表，看不到你的支付信息。

7. 儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不会有意收集任何人的数据。

8. 你的权利

由于我们不持有任何个人数据，因此没有可供导出、更正或删除的内容。卸载 App 即可清除本地数据。

9. 政策更新

如政策有变化，更新版本会发布在本页并标注新日期。

10. 联系方式

问题反馈：支持页面 · GitHub Issues
